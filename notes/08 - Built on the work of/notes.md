## Script

This builds on the work of others.

**[1]** The CDK team built the codebase all of this rests on.

**[2]** The Cashu team specified BLS blind signatures.

**[3]** And the Fedimint team showed that a federated Chaumian mint works, and made the design choices I borrowed here: BLS blind signatures, AlephBFT for consensus, and iroh as the transport. On top of that, this work adds a few new ideas.

## Background

- **Fedimint**: a federated Chaumian ecash system for Bitcoin, where a group of guardians runs a mint together.
- **AlephBFT**: a Rust library implementing asynchronous Byzantine fault tolerant consensus.
- **iroh**: a peer-to-peer networking library that connects nodes directly by public key over QUIC.
