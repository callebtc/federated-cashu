**[2]** Then a grill-me session on that spec: the agent questioned the design until the open points were settled.

**[3]** Then seven days of building with /goal. On day three there was a first working federation.

**[4]** Then one day of review.

**[5]** Then six more days of /goal, implementing the review findings.

**[6]** And since then: optimizing and fixing bugs, with no end date.
