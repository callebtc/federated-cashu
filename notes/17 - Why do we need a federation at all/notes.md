# 17 · Why do we need a federation at all

Question slide, no steps · script 66 words, about 30 s

## Script

Why do we need a federation at all? A Cashu mint today is one operator. That operator holds the key that issues ecash and the bitcoin that backs it. It can lose the funds, run off with them, refuse users, or simply go offline. A federation replaces that single operator with several independent members, so no single one of them can do any of this alone.

## Background

- **Custodial trust**: Cashu users trust the mint with the bitcoin behind their ecash; privacy comes from blind signatures, custody does not.
- **What a federation changes**: issuance and custody both need t of n members, and every operation is ordered through consensus.
